Downtown Richmond, from the Capitol to Tobacco Row, 1865: After the Evacuation Fire · isoscape

1865: After the Evacuation Fire. Retreating Confederates fire the warehouses on 2–3 April; some 35 blocks of the business district burn. An isometric view of 198 buildings standing by 1865 and ground rising from 0 to 57 m, drawn from OpenStreetMap and public elevation data.
