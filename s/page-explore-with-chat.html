Tutorial: map any place from open data with an AI chat, using Rapa Nui (Easter Island) · isoscape

A step-by-step isoscape tutorial. Ask an AI chat to find a place's open elevation and map data, check its source and license, and draw it as an isometric map. The worked example is Rapa Nui (Easter Island), drawn from Copernicus 30 m elevation and OpenStreetMap.
