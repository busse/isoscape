  January 1996 some gates were shut, Dock Street among them; in Fran, some gates."

  gravity U-wall, 43 ft from its top to the river's edge (City FAQ)."

  a sea-level figure, and "Hatched: the top of that range" can be read as 45 ft. Supported:
  "other accounts give 30 ft and 45 ft on unstated bases; ±3 ft is our judgment. Hatched:
  up to 41 ft."
