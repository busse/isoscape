**Verdict: fix. Not ready for release as it stands.** Ready for the owner's release decision

## Wall-visible faults
- **A1** Manchester, "With today's wall": the treatment-plant grounds and flats east of I-95
  drawn 20 to 22 ft under water with a gauge, while the note says the levee is unmapped and
  whether the plant is inside it is unknown; the 1986 law required the project to protect the
  plant. The picture states as a depth what the text says is unknown. (crop 06)
- **A2** Mayo Bridge on 1936, 1969, 1972, 1985: deck drawn dry over the channel, awash only
  over the island. In 1972 the water there is 12.8 ft above the stage at which the bridge is
  closed, and the research cites the bridge as inundated in Agnes. (crop 05)
- **A3** Gaston caption begins "B: the range, not a level": a leaked working name. (crop 01)
- **A4** Gaston pins against the water drawn: "13 ft up the wall" and "about 9 ft" beside
  water drawn 2 to 4 ft deep; "2 ft deep" at the market "where most water converged". The
  reports imply 24 to 35 ft; 25 to 31 is a middle figure, not "the range". (crop 09)
- **A5** Boxes 1, 2 and 7 tangle on every Shockoe sheet; moved after the look was approved. (crop 03)
- **A6** A striped slab band under the map on 1996, 2004, 1771 and the Manchester panel,
  absent on the others; it shifts the box rows on two sheets. (crops 04, 04b)
- **A7** Gaston: the river is drawn in a different blue from the key's swatch. (crop 12)
- **A8** 2020: the flood stops on a hard line across the river. (crop 07)
- **A9** 1972: gauge figures and box figures differ for the same named places (11 vs 14 ft
  at Main Street Station; 19 vs 20; 15 vs 13) with nothing saying they are different points. (crops 08, 08b)
- **A10** Box 4's fixed note is false on 1996, 2020 and nearly so on 2004 (no water in the box).
- **A11** 1771: "1 of the 6 buildings shown stand in it". (crop 02)
- **A12** Manchester 1972: box 4's frame cut by the panel edge.
- **A13** Key: basins and canal drawn in the color called "The river's own channel". (crop 10)
- Close-up only: hollow wireframe buildings on 1771 with no key entry (crop 11); a clipping
  notch on the Manchester 2020 panel.

## Facts not confirmed
1. WRONG: box 7's "1703 to 1721, from 1842 to 1913". The survey extract has 1705 at about
   1930 and 1718 at about 1950. Supported: "from about 1842 (the Anson Richards houses) to
   about 1950".
2. OVERSTATED: the Manchester plant under water, including on the real 2020 flood at 17.0 ft
   (the weather service says the plant's main access floods at 18.5 ft).
3. OVERSTATED: Gaston's "range"; pin C "about 9 ft" is derived ("6 ft 2 in inside, above a
   3 ft step"); the credit omits WTVR; pin addresses are the businesses' usual addresses.
4. OVERSTATED: Mayo Bridge levels (36.8, 31.0, 24.0) are the Locks level plus 1.3 ft assumed
   from 1969. Supported: "perhaps 1 ft higher at the Mayo Bridge (assumed from 1969)".
5. OVERSTATED: box 1 "the lowest ground of the Bottom" (box 3's center is lower).
6. OVERSTATED: Manchester box 4; supported: "a gravity U-wall, 43 ft from its top to the
   river's edge" (City FAQ).
7. OVERSTATED: 1771 "±3 ft" is our judgment (accounts run 30 to 45 ft); the sheet does not
   say where 38.0 is measured; "Great Fresh" is not in the cited source.
8. UNSOURCED: box 6 "Tobacco Row's west end".
9. Naming: Gaston was a tropical depression over Richmond while the rain fell. Supported:
   "the remnants of Gaston". Agnes, Camille and Fran as "Hurricane" confirmed as the names
   the weather service and USGS use.
10. Smaller: box 3's "reaches the James" is an inference; 2004 drops the "left out" clause;
    2020 prints "left out: 0"; the key says 2 m lidar where how-its-made says 5 m grid.

Confirmed: every level at the City Locks; every caption count against counts.json; box
depths; the wall's facts; WRDA 1986 sec. 401; the credits and byline.
