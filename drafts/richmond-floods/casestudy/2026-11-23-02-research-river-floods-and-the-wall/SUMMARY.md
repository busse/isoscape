# River floods and the floodwall: research summary

Written by an Opus 5.5 subagent, filed by the planning session. All sources read November 23,
2026. The detail and every source URL are in the files beside this one. Not yet checked
against its sources by a second reader.

## Stated with confidence
- Order by water level at the City Locks: Agnes 1972 (36.5 ft on the gauge of that era,
  35.5 ft NAVD88); the 1985 Election Day flood (30.76 ft); Camille 1969 (28.64 ft); then
  1877, 1936, 1937 and 1870 within about 2.5 ft of each other.
- By flow at Westham: Agnes 313,000 cfs, Camille 222,000, 1985 218,000, 1936 175,000.
- Where the water went before the wall: USGS mapped Camille's flood (Hydrologic Atlas HA-410):
  the Shockoe Valley floor to about Main Street Station, the canal and Tredegar ground, all of
  Mayo Island, the Manchester flats, Rocketts, and Fulton by backwater up Gillies Creek.
- Datums: at Richmond NAVD88 = NGVD29 minus 1.03 ft. Westham gauge zero 98.04 ft NAVD88. The
  City Locks zero changed twice (2.36 ft NGVD29 near Mayo's Bridge until 1956; 0.0 NGVD29 at
  the Locks from September 1957; 0.0 NAVD88 now). Agnes checks three ways at 35.5 ft NAVD88.
- Shape of the surface: nearly level below Mayo Bridge (1.3 to 1.4 ft rise over 0.9 mi from
  the Locks in 1936 and 1969), so one level per flood is defensible for Shockoe, Manchester,
  Rocketts and Fulton. Above the bridge it climbs steeply through the falls (1969: about
  10 ft at 9th Street, 14 ft more to the Lee Bridge, about 40 ft to the Boulevard Bridge).
- The wall: authorized 1986 at $91.8M, ceiling raised to $134M in 1993, 75% federal; begun
  November 5, 1988; dedicated October 21, 1994; about $143M; 17,323 ft in two parts; 750
  acres; 19 closures; 3 pump stations. Closed for floods in 1996 (twice), 1999, November 2020
  and February 2025. Highest river since: Fran 1996, about 18 ft below the NWS overtopping
  stage of 41 ft at the Locks.

## Shaky
- 1771: about 39 ft NAVD88 near Mayo's Bridge, from one Corps figure taken from diaries.
- The falls: surveyed profiles only for 1936, 1937, 1969; an Agnes sign at Tredegar reads
  40 ft NAVD88, below the 1969 profile there, which cannot be right.
- City Locks datum 2007 to 2010; Westham zero differs 0.25 ft between sources.
- The wall's design level and top elevation: no datum in any public source reached.
- FEMA flood profiles: servers refused the connection.

## Recommended floods
Agnes 1972; Camille 1969 (the calibration flood); Election Day 1985; March 1936; Fran 1996
(first test of the wall); November 2020; 1771 only as a labeled estimate. Companion idea:
Agnes's level against today's wall.

## Biggest risk
Datum bookkeeping at the City Locks: a wrong gauge zero moves a flood 1 to 2.4 ft, which is
blocks of shoreline on flat ground.
