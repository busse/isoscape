A printed book of the year's maps and cutaways, built as a Printful product for the store.
Times are Eastern. Proofs, covers and page images are kept out of git (owner's decision of
November 26 on heavy media): in the cloud workspace at `/mnt/user-data/outputs/annual/`
(with `record/`), to be moved to the Mac under `ISO-TEST/isoscape-work/annual-2026/`.
