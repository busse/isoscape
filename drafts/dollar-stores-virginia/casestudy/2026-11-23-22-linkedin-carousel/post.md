# LinkedIn post text (draft, for Chris to edit and post)

Why does it always seem like there's a Dollar General on the edge of town?

I wanted to know if that's true, so I checked it with public data. The short answer is that most
of them aren't at the edge. The ones that are sit near highway crossings on the roads into town,
which is why they're the ones you remember.

The carousel is about how we got there:
- defining "the edge of town" before counting anything
- comparing every store with random spots on the same roads
- writing the tests down first, then running them on six other states
- testing the obvious explanation (SNAP) and reporting that it didn't hold up
- mapping every case, including the ones that don't fit

I directed the work, and the research, statistics and maps were made in code with Claude. Every
step was kept, dead ends included. The full story, the poster and the sources are at
busse.github.io/isoscape/p/dollar-stores.html
