## The facts

Each fact is counted in `art.py` while the squares are drawn. None is typed in.

- **475.** Dollar General stores in Virginia that take SNAP (USDA, 2025; one row per store).
  The handoff cites the company's own figure of 499 stores in Virginia (FY2024 10-K), and the
  SNAP list misses stores that don't take SNAP. The poster says "take SNAP benefits".
- **158** of the 227 places have at least one store inside their 5 km square.
- **22** of the 184 stores in those squares stand outside the gray, and the rest are in the
  built-up town. A store counts as inside if it is inside the gray of the square it is drawn
  in, which matches what the reader sees. This agrees with the editorial poster's main
  finding: most stores are in town.

A quick separate count, made before the panels, gave the same numbers: 475, 184, 158 and 22.
