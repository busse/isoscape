# Review, November 2026

*About this page: it was written with AI. Claude, a model made by Anthropic, drafted it from the
review described here, and I read and approved it. — Chris*

On November 16, 2026 I had every map on this site looked at closely, at about the size it would
be printed. Until then I had only checked a few maps that way, and those few had real faults. This
page says what the review found and what I am doing about it.

## How the review was done

All 92 published maps were rendered at about print scale. For each one, the whole sheet was looked
at, then three to seven close crops: the place the map is named for, the densest buildings,
bridges, water edges and steep ground. The looking was done by Claude, working through the maps in
four batches, with a picture saved for every fault. It found 218 faults. Of those, 87 are things
you would notice on a wall. No map came through clean. Each map got only a few close crops, so
there are certainly more.

## What it found

- **Landmarks that do not show their landmark.** On about 41 maps the thing in the title is
  missing, drawn as a plain block, or the wrong shape. The Statue of Liberty map has a pedestal
  and no statue. The Jefferson Memorial is a spiral of walls with no dome. The Washington Monument
  has no point. Niagara Falls has no drop. isoscape draws buildings by raising their mapped
  outline to a height, and that is not enough for a monument, an arch or a waterfall.
- **Rocks where there are none.** The rule that finds rocks and rapids in the James also puts
  them on calm water and lawns: San Francisco Bay, Lake Mead, Crater Lake, a lawn at the Lincoln
  Memorial. About 25 maps.
- **Bridges that end in the air.** Ramps and decks stop short, float as stubs, or break into
  pieces. About 28 maps, including most of the Richmond years.
- **Old Richmond with today's streets.** In the dated Richmond views only the buildings are
  drawn for the year. The streets, parking lots, highway ramps, the Mayo Bridge and the ground
  itself are today's, even in 1737. The 1788 Capitol has wings that were built in 1904–06. Some
  buildings traced from old maps stand in what is now water.
- **Smaller things.** Blocky hill shading with faint stripes. Pieces of tall buildings floating
  above their towers. Houses on steep hills drawn as tall as towers. Lakes missing from Central
  Park. The portico of Broad Street Station drawn with uneven columns.

## What I did about it

Only work I believe is accurate should be for sale. So on November 16 I took all 97 designs out
of the shop. First went every landmark, every dated Richmond view and composite, and both sets
(Richmond's Streetcars, 1925 and Broad Street Station, 1943), because their sheets include views
with these faults. That left four present-day pieces: The James, Richmond 2026, Richmond and the
James, and Gwynn's Island. Those were then looked at tile by tile, the whole sheet at print size.
None was clean, so they came out too.

The maps stay here in the explorer as they are, faults included. This is a place to see the work
as it stands.

## What is being fixed

Richmond comes first, and Gwynn's Island with it.

- **Done, not yet redrawn:** smooth hill shading with no stripes; the Broad Street Station
  portico; stray dots and lines on the Eiffel Tower.
- **In progress:** rocks and rapids only where a river really falls; missing lakes and pools;
  bridges and ramps that end properly; floating building parts; houses on hills.
- **In progress, and harder:** drawing streets, bridges, water and ground for the year, not just
  buildings. Every dated rule will name its source, or say that it is a guess.
- **A check that runs every time.** Each new drawing is now compared with the last approved one,
  crop by crop, so a change in one place cannot quietly break another.
- **On hold:** the landmarks. Most need their subject modeled part by part from sources, the way
  Broad Street Station is. I will bring them back one at a time, when each is right.

When a piece is fixed it will be redrawn as a new version, listed on the [Changes](changes.md)
page, and put back in the shop.
