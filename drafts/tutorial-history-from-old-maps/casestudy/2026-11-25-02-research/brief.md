# Research brief: the four corners of Broad and Belvidere (November 25, 2026)

Every source below was opened in this session. Scans and raw downloads are on the Mac in
`ISO-TEST/isoscape-work/tutorial-history-from-old-maps/research/`.

## The Sanborn sheets

- Richmond's 1924–25 Sanborn is in four volumes at the Library of Congress: vol. 1 (downtown,
  sanborn09064_006, 1924, sheets 1–73), vol. 2 (East End, _007), vol. 3 (north, _008), vol. 4
  (west, _009). The 1952 reissues are _016 (vol. 1, "Vol. 1, 1924; Republished 1952", 78 files)
  and _017, _018. LoC states its online Sanborn collection is public domain.
- The vol. 1 key map puts the intersection on **sheets 33 and 34**, at 50 feet to the inch:
  - **sheet 33**: the two corners west of Belvidere (NW block 600–612 W. Broad, bounded by
    Munford, Smith and Marshall; SW block 601–619 W. Broad, to Grace and Pine);
  - **sheet 34**: the two corners east (NE block 500–524 W. Broad, to Henry and Marshall; SE block
    501–531 W. Broad, to Henry and Grace).
  Sheets 27 and 28 were downloaded too but aren't needed. Both editions have the same sheet
  numbers, so 4 scans in all: 1924-0033, 1924-0034, 1952-0033, 1952-0034.
- None of these sheets has been traced before. isoscape's downtown traces run from sheet 1 to
  24, and the Jackson Ward traces are in vol. 3.
- **On the corners in 1924:** an auto row. On the SW corner, auto sales and repairs, an
  undertaker, and a service station with gasoline tanks at 619. On the NW, auto top repair,
  paints and oils, and furniture storage, with the 2nd Precinct police station behind on
  Marshall. On the NE, paints, auto repair, a gas-and-oil drive at 516, a bicycle shop, and a
  drugstore at 502. On the SE, vulcanizing and clothes-pressing shops and a service station.
  St. Elizabeth Hospital and Grace Hospital are a block south.
- **In 1952:** still autos. The SW corner is one large auto sales building, "steel cols & beams",
  with a filling station on the corner. The NW still has stores.
- **Street name:** north of Broad, the street at the corner is labeled "Smith" on both
  editions. Today it is North Belvidere Street, US 1 and 301. Whether the road was widened on
  Smith's line or cut on a new one should show when the sheet is laid on today's ground. This
  needs checking, not assuming.

## Claims to check (good teaching cases)

- A guest commentary (Dotts and Kerr, Richmond BizSense, April 3, 2026) says VCU's purchase
  of 409–423 W. Broad "solidifies VCU's essential control of all four corners". The July 2026
  assessor record lists the NE corner under a private owner. The purchase address is a block
  east of the corner. Find out what was meant before quoting it.
- Virginia Business (April 25, 2017) says the ICA site "was once the site of a small train
  station". The 1924 sheet shows the Richmond-Ashland Railway's (electric) station at
  812–816 W. Broad, two blocks west on the north side, and auto businesses on the ICA block.
  Check the earlier editions (1905, 1919) before saying anything.
- Moving the scan to today's ground has to account for the Belvidere/Smith alignment.
