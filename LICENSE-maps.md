# Licences for maps and data

**Code.** The isoscape code is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). Use it for anything.

**Maps you make.** A map you render with isoscape is yours. Print it, sell it, share it. It must
credit OpenStreetMap wherever it is shown (see below); isoscape writes that credit into every SVG
and page.

**Chris Busse's published maps.** The views on the isoscape site, in `docs/`, and the print files
are © Chris Busse, licensed [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): share
and adapt them with credit, but not for sale. Prints of them are in the holiday shop at
[chrisbusse.com](https://chrisbusse.com) through December 31, 2026.

**Data.**
- Map data © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, under the
  [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). The cached `data/*/osm.json`
  files are OSM data and carry that licence. A rendered map is a produced work: credit OSM.
- Elevation: [USGS 3D Elevation Program](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program), public domain.
- Land cover: [NLCD](https://www.mrlc.gov/), public domain. Building footprints where OSM has few:
  [Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) (ODbL).
- Historic Richmond: buildings traced from [Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps](https://www.loc.gov/collections/sanborn-maps/)
  in the Library of Congress (public domain); year-built from City of Richmond open data.
