Hearst Castle — San Simeon, California

How this view was made

Hearst Castle — San Simeon, California. A frame 1.8 km across and 2.34 km deep (4.21 km²), centered on 35.6853°N, 121.1675°W.

Camera how the camera works →

Looking north-northeast (bearing 30°), turned 30.4° clockwise from the configured 0° so the street grid runs along the diagonals. Orthographic, tilted 30° down from the horizon: ground running into the picture is drawn at 0.5× its length and heights at 0.87×. 3.333 px per meter across the frame; the whole drawing is 6,047 × 5,271 px at 1:1.

Sources where the data comes from →

  • OpenStreetMap: 152 features read from the cached download.
  • USGS 3DEP elevation (United States (U.S. Geological Survey); bare-earth lidar, 1 m across most of the US, served by the National Map), fetched at 2 m per pixel and resampled to a 5 m grid. The ground runs from 103.4 m to 491.0 m above sea level (387.6 m of relief), drawn 2× taller than life. Buildings and bridges keep their true heights.

Ground what the ground is made of →

From the map, 7 mapped areas of water, islands, parks, woods and other cover, and 75 ribbons of road, path, rail, canal and weir. Painted in 234 strips from back to front, 10 m deep each, so hills hide what is behind them (why). Contours every 2 m with every 5th line heavier: 225,018 segments.

Rivers and rapids how rapids are read →

No stretch of water read as rapids: places where the river’s surface, recovered from the lidar, falls faster than 0 m per 100 m, measured over 40 m. Rock standing more than 1 m clear of that surface is drawn as boulders. Both are inferred from the lidar, not observed.

Buildings where heights come from →

24 buildings, extruded from footprints of at least 12 m² that lie wholly inside the frame, from OpenStreetMap.

  • 0 (0%) from a height tag
  • 2 (8%) from the number of floors, at 3.4 m a floor
  • 22 (92%) guessed from the building’s type and size

Bridges how decks are placed →

No bridges in the frame.

Drawing paint order →

The paper palette, a dot on every visible corner, on a ground slab 12 m thick.
