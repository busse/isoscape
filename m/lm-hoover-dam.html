Hoover Dam — Nevada and Arizona

Explore this map

Drawn by Chris Busse with isoscape, a free, open-source tool he built with Claude, from OpenStreetMap and public elevation data; how it's made. In the explorer you can pan and zoom the full drawing and compare it with other maps.

How this view was made

Hoover Dam — Nevada and Arizona. A frame 2.6 km across and 3.38 km deep (8.79 km²), centered on 36.0157°N, 114.7375°W.

Camera how the camera works →

Looking north (bearing 0°), a fixed bearing with no snapping. Orthographic, tilted 30° down from the horizon: ground running into the picture is drawn at 0.5× its length and heights at 0.87×. 2.308 px per meter across the frame; the whole drawing is 6,049 × 4,735 px at 1:1.

Sources where the data comes from →

  • OpenStreetMap: 378 features read from the cached download.
  • USGS 3DEP elevation (United States (U.S. Geological Survey); bare-earth lidar, 1 m across most of the US, served by the National Map), fetched at 2 m per pixel and resampled to a 5 m grid. The ground runs from 196.5 m to 693.7 m above sea level (497.2 m of relief), drawn 1× taller than life. Buildings and bridges keep their true heights.

Ground what the ground is made of →

From the map, 26 mapped areas of water, islands, parks, woods and other cover, and 225 ribbons of road, path, rail, canal and weir. Painted in 338 strips from back to front, 10 m deep each, so hills hide what is behind them (why). Contours every 5 m with every 5th line heavier: 262,245 segments.

Rivers and rapids how rapids are read →

No stretch of water read as rapids: places where the river’s surface, recovered from the lidar, falls faster than 0 m per 100 m, measured over 40 m. Rock standing more than 1 m clear of that surface is drawn as boulders. Both are inferred from the lidar, not observed.

Buildings where heights come from →

34 buildings, extruded from footprints of at least 12 m² that lie wholly inside the frame, from OpenStreetMap.

  • 0 (0%) from a height tag
  • 1 (3%) from the number of floors, at 3.4 m a floor
  • 33 (97%) guessed from the building’s type and size

Bridges how decks are placed →

254 bridge spans of up to 22 m, so long bridges sort among the buildings. Deck heights are rules of thumb: every bridge 7 m per OSM layer.

Drawing paint order →

The paper palette, a dot on every visible corner, on a ground slab 17 m thick.
